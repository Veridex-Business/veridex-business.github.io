The fee

Pricing

You pay for the work done. We send a Revolut Business invoice with a secure payment link by WhatsApp or email. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Revolut transfer through that link. Households pay before or at the time of the job. Business customers pay within 7 days of the invoice. A one-off job does not need a contract.

We will never ask for card details by phone, message or email; only pay through the secure link on our invoice.
