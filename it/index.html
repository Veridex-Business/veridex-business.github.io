Questions

FAQ

Is there a long contract?

No. Before anything starts, I will write down the fee, what is included, and how much notice either of us gives to stop. You can read that and decide. I do not sign people up for a year.

What areas do you cover?

Ireland. The work is mostly remote, which suits inboxes, automations, and day-to-day IT. If a visit would genuinely help, we can talk about whether that is practical for where you are.

What if something breaks?

Email me and I will look at it. Everyday IT support is part of the monthly fee. If a problem is outside what we agreed, I will say so before doing extra work.
