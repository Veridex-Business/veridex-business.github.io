How we use AI to handle email

We use an AI assistant to help sort incoming email and to draft replies. A person at Veridex reads and checks every AI-drafted reply before it is sent, and replies that were drafted with AI say so. Outside working hours, you may get a fixed automatic reply. It is not written by AI. No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you by automated means. We never put our clients' job data, or health or other sensitive information, into AI tools.

The AI assistant is Cursor / Grok Bot (Anysphere, Inc., USA). We use it with its Privacy Mode switched on. Anysphere states that in Privacy Mode your data is not used to train AI models, and that the AI model providers it uses (SpaceXAI, OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta) have zero-data-retention agreements, so they do not store or train on it. An exception: messages flagged by abuse-detection systems may be kept for investigation.

Grok Bot may retain customer data, files, browser sessions, credentials, memory, and routines in a persistent cloud environment until we delete them. Anysphere publishes its list of sub-processors at trust.cursor.com/subprocessors.

Website

This site has no analytics, no tracking cookies and no contact form. Our host, GitHub, logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, under its own privacy statement (GitHub privacy statement).

Nothing on the 3D printed products page is for sale yet. When the shop opens, we will collect the details needed to take and fulfil an order, such as name, delivery address, email, and what you ordered. Card payments will be taken by Stripe.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Customer records and correspondence: for the length of the work, then 6 years after the end of the financial year they relate to. Revenue requires business records to be kept for six years. Records may be kept longer if needed for a legal claim.
  • AI working copies (drafts, summaries and copies of email in the AI assistant): deleted within 30 days.
  • Website logs are kept by GitHub under its own privacy statement. We cannot see or delete them, and we do not receive them.

We do not put you on a mailing list because you wrote to us. We do not rely on consent for ordinary enquiry emails. If we ever need consent, for example for optional marketing, we will ask clearly and you can withdraw it.
