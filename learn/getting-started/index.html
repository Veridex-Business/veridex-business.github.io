Lesson 1: Getting started

What you'll learn

How to find your way around your phone or computer, and how to get apps (programs on your phone) safely.

Steps

1. You can't break it by trying

Tapping around is how everyone learns. If you get lost, you can always go back. Just don't tap anything that says Reset, Erase or Delete unless you're sure.

2. Find the "back" and "home" buttons

The back button takes you one step back. The home button takes you to the start screen. On many phones, you swipe up from the bottom instead.

3. Make the writing bigger

Open Settings (the gear or cog picture). Look for "Display" or "Text size". Move the slider until the words are easy to read.

4. Get apps only from the official app store

The official app store is the shop built into your phone. On an iPhone it's called the App Store. On most other phones it's called Google Play. Apps there are checked, but a fake can still slip in, so look at the app's name and who made it before you install.

5. Close pop-ups that say "Download now!"

A pop-up (a box that suddenly appears on the screen) is not the app store. If it tells you to download something, close it or go back.

Try it

Open your official app store. Search for a weather app. Look at its name and who made it. You don't need to install it. Then go back to your home screen.

Stop, Check, Ask

If a message, call or pop-up rushes you, or asks for money or details:

  1. Stop. Don't tap anything or reply straight away.
  2. Check another way. Use a phone number you already trust.
  3. Ask someone you trust, like a family member or friend.

Printable summary

Getting Started

  • Tapping around won't break it. Avoid Reset, Erase and Delete.
  • The back button takes you one step back.
  • The home button takes you to the start.
  • Make the writing bigger in Settings.
  • An app is a program on your phone.

  • A pop-up saying "Download now!" is not the app store. Close it.
