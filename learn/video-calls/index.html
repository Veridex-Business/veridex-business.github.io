Lesson 4: Video calls with family

What you'll learn

How to answer and make a video call, so you can see the people you love.

Steps

1. Use an app you trust

Many families already use a video call app. Ask your family which one they use. If you need it, get it from the official app store.

2. Answer a call

When a video call comes in, tap the green button. To end the call, tap the red button.

3. Turn your camera and sound on or off

Look for a small camera picture and a microphone picture (the microphone picks up your voice). Tap them to turn them on or off.

4. Get comfortable

Hold your phone at face height, or lean it against something. Sit facing a window or a lamp, so your family can see you clearly.

5. Only answer people you know

If a stranger video calls you, you don't need to answer. Just let it ring.

Try it

Ask a family member to video call you at a set time. Answer it. Turn your camera off and on again. Then say goodbye and end the call.

Stop, Check, Ask

If a message, call or pop-up rushes you, or asks for money or details:

  1. Stop. Don't tap anything or reply straight away.
  2. Check another way. Use a phone number you already trust.
  3. Ask someone you trust, like a family member or friend.

Printable summary

Video Calls and Staying Safe

  • Tap the green button to answer. Tap the red button to end.
  • Tap the camera or microphone picture to turn them on or off.
  • Hold your phone at face height.
  • Only answer video calls from people you know.
  • Get apps only from the official app store.
  • Odd message? Stop, Check, Ask.
  • Only you type your PIN and passwords.
  • Not sure? Ring the number on the back of your bank card.
