Help your parents with tech

Simple, calm lessons for older people, and for the family helping them.

Who this is for

  • Anyone who feels unsure about their phone or computer.
  • Family and friends who want to help.

You don't need to know anything to start.

How to use these lessons

  1. Do one lesson at a time.
  2. Have your phone or computer beside you.
  3. Read each step, then try it yourself.
  4. Go slowly. It's fine to read a step twice.
  5. Print the summary at the end of each lesson and keep it handy.

Each lesson takes about 15 to 20 minutes to read and try.

Helping a parent? Sit beside them and let them tap and type. Never type their passwords or PIN for them.

The 4 lessons

  1. Getting started: your phone or computer, and getting apps safely.
  2. Spotting scam messages: what to do when a message feels odd.
  3. Passwords and safe banking: keeping your accounts locked.
  4. Video calls with family: seeing the people you love.

Stop, Check, Ask

If a message, call or pop-up rushes you, or asks for money or details:

  1. Stop. Don't tap anything or reply straight away.
  2. Check another way. Use a phone number you already trust.
  3. Ask someone you trust, like a family member or friend.
