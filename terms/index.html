2. What we do and don't do

What we do:

  • software and account setup;
  • email;
  • backups and restore tests;
  • everyday security;
  • simple automations;
  • lessons;
  • the jobs listed on our IT page, for households and small businesses.

Remote first. We do most work remotely, anywhere in Ireland. On-site visits are in Limerick city only, by arrangement. We don't travel further.

What we don't do:

  • hardware repair (we don't open or fix devices, screens, batteries or circuit boards);
  • buying hardware or software for you (you buy it in your own name and we set it up);
  • work on systems you don't own or have permission to use.

If a job turns out to be outside what we do, we tell you and stop. You pay only for the time already agreed and used (section 4).

How time is charged:

If we can't tell how long it will take, we say how it's charged (the hourly rate and steps above) and the most we'll charge without asking you again.

4. Booking and payment

  • Households: card upfront. When you agree the quote, we send you a Revolut Business payment link for the fixed price, or for the agreed number of hours. We start once it's paid. We don't take card details ourselves.
  • If the job takes less time than you paid for, we refund the difference to the same card within 14 days of finishing.
  • If it needs more time, we stop and ask first. Extra time is charged only if you agree in writing, and we send a new link for it. No surprise charges.

How to spot a genuine payment link:

  • It comes from us during a job you've agreed.
  • It opens on Revolut's own site.
  • It shows Veridex Ltd and the amount and reference we gave you.
  • We never ask you to pay a new bank account, pay in gift cards or crypto, or send card details by email, text or WhatsApp.
  • If anything looks different, ring us on 085 715 0506 before paying.

Business customers are invoiced, and payment is due within 7 days.

Home Care plan (€19 a month): paid monthly in advance by a Revolut Business payment link. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice. No rollover of unused time.

5. Remote access

  • We connect to your device only with your agreement each time, using RustDesk. You start or approve the session.
  • You can watch everything we do, and end the session at any time by closing the tool or telling us to stop.
  • We use only the access the job needs. When the job is done we remove any access we were given (accounts, admin rights, remote tools), unless you ask us in writing to keep it for an agreed purpose, such as Home Care.
  • We don't keep copies of your files or passwords after the job, except where the job is to copy them (for example, a data transfer).

6. What we need from you

  • Back up first. Make sure you have a recent copy of anything important before we start, unless the job is to set up or check your backups. We'll help you check this if you ask.
  • Licensed software only. We install or set up only software you're licensed to use.
  • Permission. You confirm that you own the devices and accounts we work on, or have the owner's permission.
  • No passwords by email. Don't send passwords, codes or card details by email, text or WhatsApp. We'll show you a safer way (for example, typing them in yourself during the session).
  • Tell us about anything that could affect the job (for example, a device that's failing, or files you can't lose).

7. If you're a household customer (consumer)

7.1 Your rights

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 (Part 4):

  • we must have the skill to do the job, and do it with reasonable care and skill;
  • what we tell you about the service binds us, if you rely on it;
  • we must do the work within the time agreed, or within a reasonable time;
  • if no price was fixed, you pay a reasonable price and no more.

If the work isn't right, you can ask us to put it right free of charge, within a reasonable time. If we can't or don't, or the problem is serious, you can have a price reduction or end the contract (and get a refund for what wasn't done properly).

7.2 Your 14-day right to cancel

  • When it applies: if you book with us by phone, email, text, WhatsApp or online (a distance contract), or away from our premises (for example, at your home), you can cancel within 14 days of agreeing the booking, without giving a reason.
  • Starting sooner: if you want us to start within those 14 days, you must ask us to, in writing (a reply to our quote saying so is enough). You also confirm that you understand you lose the right to cancel once the work is fully done.
  • If you cancel part-way: you pay only for the work done up to the time you tell us, in proportion to the agreed price. We refund the rest within 14 days, to the card you paid with.
  • If you cancel before we start: we refund everything.
  • How to cancel: tell us clearly by email or phone (details in section 1), or use the form at the end of this page. The deadline is met if you send it before the 14 days are up.

7.3 Your statutory rights

Nothing in these terms takes away or limits your rights under consumer law. For advice, contact the CCPC (section 12).

10. Your personal data

  • Veridex Ltd is the controller of the personal data we collect to deal with you: your contact details, messages, bookings, invoices and payment records.

11. How we use AI

  • No decision about you is made by automated means. More in our privacy notice.
