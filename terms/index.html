Our prices are on our IT page and include VAT at 23%.

4. Payment

We send a Revolut Business invoice with a secure payment link by WhatsApp or email. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Revolut transfer through that link. Households pay before or at the time of the job. Business customers pay within 7 days of the invoice.

We will never ask for card details by phone, message or email; only pay through the secure link on our invoice.

  • Less time than paid for: if an hourly job takes less time than you paid for, we refund the difference.
  • More time: we charge for extra time only if you agree to it before we do it.
  • Home Care plan: €19 per month, paid monthly in advance through that invoice link. It runs month to month. You can end it at any time, and it ends at the end of the month you have paid for.

5. Right to cancel (households buying for personal use)

You have the right to cancel this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The cancellation period will expire after 14 days from the day of the conclusion of the contract.

To exercise the right to cancel, you must inform us (Veridex Ltd, Unit 2, 2 Bridge Street, Athlone, Co. Westmeath, N37 F1W4, contact@veridex.ie) of your decision to cancel this contract by an unequivocal statement such as an e-mail or a letter sent by post. You may use the attached cancellation form but it is not obligatory.

To meet the cancellation deadline, it is sufficient for you to send your communication concerning your exercise of the right to cancel before the cancellation period has expired.

Effects of cancellation. If you cancel this contract, we will reimburse to you all payments received from you, without undue delay and in any event not later than 14 days from the day on which we are informed of your decision to cancel this contract. We will carry out such reimbursement using the same means of payment as you used for the initial transaction, unless you have expressly agreed otherwise; in any event, you will not incur any fees as a result of such reimbursement.

If you requested to begin the performance of services during the cancellation period, you shall pay us an amount which is in proportion to what has been provided until you have communicated to us your cancellation of this contract, in comparison with the full coverage of the contract.

When the right is lost. If you ask us to start within the 14 days, you lose the right to cancel once the service has been fully supplied.
